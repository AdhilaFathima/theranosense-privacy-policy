Privacy Policy

Theranosense Infusion Control

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Theranosense Infusion Control handles information when you use the application.

Theranosense Infusion Control is designed to provide control and monitoring functions for a connected infusion-control prototype. The application is intended primarily for development, testing, and research purposes.

1. Information the App Handles

The application may send and receive operational information related to the connected infusion-control system. This may include:

  • Configured flow rate
  • Configured volume
  • Infusion control status
  • Pause, resume, start, and stop commands
  • Operational measurements received from connected hardware
  • Device or connection status required for communication with the system

This information is used to operate and monitor the connected prototype and is not intended to identify individual users.

2. Personal Information

Theranosense Infusion Control does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, address, telephone number, or email address in order to use the application.

The application is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from users.

3. How Information Is Used

Operational information handled by the application is used only for functions such as:

  • Controlling the connected infusion system
  • Displaying operational parameters
  • Monitoring system status
  • Providing alerts related to configured operating conditions
  • Testing and evaluating the prototype

4. Data Sharing

Theranosense Infusion Control does not sell, rent, or intentionally share users' personal information with third parties.

The application does not intentionally transmit personal information to external advertising or marketing services.

5. Network and Connected Hardware

The application may communicate with a connected control device, such as a Raspberry Pi, over a local network. This communication is used to send control commands and receive operational information from the connected infusion-control prototype.

The application does not require this operational communication to be used for advertising or user profiling.

6. Data Storage and Retention

The application does not intentionally maintain a user account or a personal profile.

Operational information is used as necessary for the application's control and monitoring functions. Where information is temporarily processed during operation, it is not intended to be retained as a personal record.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to limit the handling of information to what is necessary for the application's intended functionality.

Users should operate the application and connected hardware only on networks and equipment that they trust and control.

8. Third-Party Services

The application does not intentionally use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect personal information.

If third-party services are added to the application in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to describe their data practices.

9. Children's Privacy

The application is not directed specifically toward children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application or its data practices. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
